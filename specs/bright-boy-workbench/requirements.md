# Requirements: 5BX Workout Mixer photographic preview prototype

Status: **Revised draft — awaiting Charles's approval before implementation**
Owner: Charles
Last updated: 2026-11-25

## Decision and goal

Charles confirmed that the existing **5BX Workout Mixer** is the correct tool. The
immediate goal is a working review-and-cosmetics prototype, **not** a pose editor.
It should show the movements and the latest photo/video experiments in place,
reuse the Mixer controls that already work, and demonstrate how a genuinely
transparent photographic cutout could be put over a different background.

The current Mixer source is `sprites/kit/demo/page.html`, generated by
`sprites/kit/make_demo.py`. The published build is separate from the `main`
branch. Its existing stage uses transparent **cartoon** Sprite Boy strips and
already supports chart/exercise selection, playback, backdrop/prop toggles, and
shorts/shoes/tank recoloring. The newer **photographic** middle-school avatar
and experimental pose cards/videos are separate and are not production assets.

## First increment — requirements

### R1. Keep the working controls

1. The Mixer SHALL retain its production cartoon animation, chart and exercise
   selection, play/pause, speed, background layers, props, and hue presets.
2. Existing video previews and source artwork SHALL remain unchanged.
3. No data, exercise timing, or form rules in `data/5bx-charts.json` SHALL change.
4. If files are missing from a local checkout, the build/review SHALL report
   their absence honestly; it SHALL NOT silently substitute generated art.

### R2. See current photographic tests inline

1. The Mixer SHALL add a clearly marked **Photographic experiments — not
   approved production art** panel, separate from the current Sprite Boy stage.
2. It SHALL support browser-local preview of selected PNG/JPG/WebP images and
   MP4/WebM videos, without uploading or altering the imported files. Images
   remain visible at full aspect ratio; videos retain native pause/scrub.
3. Its exercise inventory SHALL show the movements by chart/exercise and let
   Charles inspect the current stills, complete multi-pose cards, and videos
   where they exist. Missing media SHALL say **not yet available**, not imply
   that all movements have already been generated or verified.
4. The panel SHALL expose a review index and source links for the numbered
   photographic cards and video QA, as available. A link to private Drive
   content is not necessarily an embeddable media URL.
5. An image/video SHALL never be labeled approved solely because it was opened
   in the viewer. Where the catalog has diagnostic or rejected takes, the
   text SHALL reflect that status.
6. When no local media is chosen, the panel SHALL offer a clear import action
   and review-index links—not a broken or fabricated thumbnail.

### R3. Test a genuinely transparent photographic sprite

1. A cutout experiment SHALL use a named existing photographic source frame
   and produce an **RGBA PNG with alpha**, not a white/black/checkerboard
   background baked into its pixels.
2. Its transparency SHALL be machine-checked; the result SHALL be visually
   compared with the source for identity, anatomy, pose, outfit, and halo/fringe
   quality. A generated new likeness is **not** equivalent to perfect pixel
   isolation and SHALL be recorded as experimental.
3. The Mixer SHALL allow imported alpha PNGs to be previewed over at least
   three user-selectable visual backgrounds (for example a dark stage, light
   studio, and simple track-color scene) using CSS or canvas compositing.
4. Plain photographs with opaque backgrounds SHALL also display, but their
   original track/trees SHALL NOT be falsely described as replaceable layers.
5. Any production-ready animated photographic character would require
   individual, registered, consistently anchored cutouts for the relevant
   frames. This increment proves a **single-frame** swap, not all movements.

### R4. Cosmetic controls without anatomy distortion

1. Existing hue sliders SHALL continue to recolor **only** known cartoon
   sprite garment regions, as they do today.
2. The photographic preview SHALL NOT run a global hue filter that affects
   skin, face, hair, or background. Mask-based photographic recoloring is a
   separate future task, not a working capability in this increment.
3. The photographic boy SHALL retain a plausible 13–14-year-old appearance:
   fair skin, modest messy dark hair, undeveloped/soft normal proportions,
   no pronounced muscle definition or facial/body hair. Source masters are
   references, not mutable production registrations.

### R5. Testable, safe handoff

1. The prototype SHALL stay static/browser-local, need no backend login, and
   not consume image/video generation credits during UI interactions.
2. The implementation SHALL have tests or named manual checks for existing
   Mixer controls, image/video import, alpha-versus-opaque background behavior,
   missing media, responsive viewing, and no upload/network writes.
3. Existing `sprites/tests` SHALL continue to pass.
4. The update SHALL be prepared as a reviewable GitHub feature branch/PR,
   separate from live Cloudflare Pages. No merge, production art registration,
   or deployment without Charles's explicit review.

## Not in this prototype

- No rig, limb/joint manipulation, pose dragging, inverse kinematics, or new
  frame synthesis from the Mixer.
- No requirement to build all motion cycles as transparent photographic strips
  now; the complete-movement photographic/video review remains read-only.
- No automatic replacement of the original cyan/red cartoon Sprite Boy with
  the photographic boy.
- No uploading experimental raw files to Git or automatically approving them.
- No automatic pose or identity correction from a preview.

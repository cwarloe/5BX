Editorial decisions captured here

White-background exercise PNGs remain the approved movement references, not the planned finished backdrop. The intended finished footage uses the same small cream-wall gym in every exercise, with a consistent child-sized physical scale and distance, adjusted only by real position within the studio—not arbitrary enlargement per pose. Raised arms may crop above frame while the head remains visible. Exercise/interstitial cards and the title/tip and timer are editable HTML overlays, not baked into video. Enlarged dark-green titles and tips appear top-left on the cream backdrop, the circular countdown top-right, and small frame numbers bottom-right for storyboard review only.

The first solo toe-touch gym composite was too squat through the torso. Candidate v2 replaces it in the active preview, regenerated from the original approved character master with the five-pose size reference; the first image remains linked for comparison. The revised still is awaiting motion QA, not a finished gym video. Background jitter checks will use the left blind/cabinet, wall edge, both dumbbell racks, and two floor weights. Title-card length, end-card length, and the scissor burst's exact placement within six minutes remain provisional. The 2/1/1/1/6 exercise timing is from the 5BX source, and scissor bursts are triggered by counted steps, not arbitrary seconds.

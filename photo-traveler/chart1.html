The working sequence

1. VerifyUse the selected white card, each high-res pose, and the existing motion clip.
2. Motion proofReuse a working white clip; only generate if the motion is genuinely missing.
3. Gym renderKeep the working pose sequence and camera. Add the static CFC gym as the only scene change.
4. FinishReturn the gym MP4; select the invisible three-rep loop seam, extend duration, add editable title/timer, and review.

Shared production assets

Reference links go to Google Drive. The primary card and individual PNGs are the upload packet; older diagnostic cards are collapsed under Archive. The copyable prompt is the next gym-render proposal, not a claim of the exact wording originally used in Flow. When a successful source video exists, it governs motion and camera framing.

Final assembly · once loops are usable

Use the existing gym background with the green wall, centered Charles Fit Club brand, and olive mat. Standing moves happen in front of the mat and fill more of the frame; floor moves happen on the mat. Keep Mark's scale fixed within each exercise. Place the editable white-stencil exercise name/tip at top left and white circular timer at top right—no text border.

Quick visual QA

  • Correct Mark (bright blue sleeveless shirt), stable identity, natural shoes/feet.
  • Motion matches the card, with clear limb ownership and a plausible range.
  • Three visible repetitions; no melting limbs or visible loop snap.
  • Consistent camera angle, size, gym light, foreground mat, and white title/timer placement.
  • Finished MP4 is presented to Charles for acceptance; a still or rough workprint is not a finished approval.

The 5BX photographic assets are production references, not independently verified exercise instruction. Status here tracks media readiness, not medical/form certification.

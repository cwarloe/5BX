Final assembly · once loops are usable

Use the existing gym background with the green wall, centered Charles Fit Club brand, and olive mat. Standing moves happen in front of the mat and fill more of the frame; floor moves happen on the mat. Keep Mark's scale fixed within each exercise. Place the editable white-stencil exercise name/tip at top left and white circular timer at top right—no text border.

Quick visual QA

  • Correct Mark (bright blue sleeveless shirt), stable identity, natural shoes/feet.
  • Motion matches the card, with clear limb ownership and a plausible range.
  • Three visible repetitions; no melting limbs or visible loop snap.
  • Consistent camera angle, size, gym light, foreground mat, and white title/timer placement.
  • Finished MP4 is presented to Charles for acceptance; a still or rough workprint is not a finished approval.

## Booklet-specific mechanics — acceptance gates

1. **ID35 twist requires a *contralateral* elbow/knee pairing.** Source: back lying, straight legs together, hands clasped behind head; sit up as a leg bends, twist *right elbow to left knee*, then alternate the direction in the next repetition. The generated B/C are photographically coherent, but show essentially the same raised-knee silhouette and no convincing axial trunk reversal. This is the largest Chart 5 failure and a **hard REJECT independent of photographic quality**. If retried, require distinct physical leg identity and opposite shoulder rotation; ankle color tags are useful diagnostics, but markers must be removed by a separately validated edit. Keep the body's ordinary range of motion rather than morphing elbow length or artificially rotating the spine.
2. **ID36 overhead arms stay overhead, not a lateral T.** All lifted frames satisfy this. Both feet lift in C with knees straight-ish, no extreme lumbar hyperextension. Better a restrained valid arch than a high-arching adult gymnast.
3. **ID37 clap requires a moment when *both palms are unsupported*, a visible contact clap, then replacement on floor.** B and C pass these still-frame conditions. A/C/D transitions and C's unsupported chest remain a physical-timing risk; for an ordinary middle-school character do not suggest holding C. A prompt for a *single rapid clap at apex, hands immediately returning* may be better than asking for a static heroic pose. Hands meeting in a photo is not video evidence of an actual clap motion.
4. **ID38 semi-spread-eagle requires astride legs and hands swung overhead *in mid-air***, with feet together, hands on knees on landing. B/C meet the airborne test, D resets. The source row trails off after an incomplete “Raise”; no extra requirement should be inferred from that fragment.

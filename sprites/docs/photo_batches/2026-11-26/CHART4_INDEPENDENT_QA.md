## Exact-booklet gates / lessons

- **Ex2:** Supine, straight *arms overhead and legs together* at start; toes reached through a sit-up with **both arms and legs straight**. Prioritize honest adolescent flexibility over arm stretching or anatomy morphing. This is the only minor technical deviation in ID30.
- **Ex3:** Prone, **both arms extended straight laterally**, raising head/shoulders/chest, **both legs**. Raised arms alongside hips are a different movement; B/C in ID31 do not exhibit that failure.
- **Ex4:** Hands **about one foot sideways from ears**, fully extended at top and both elbows bent at bottom. A normal narrow military push-up does not satisfy this; ID32 is visibly wide, with precise feet/inches unprovable.
- **Jump:** Half crouch, straightish back, hands on knees, **right foot slightly in front**, then jump to an upright airborne position. Screen left/right should never be equated to anatomical left/right without tracing individual limbs. ID33 demonstrates jump mechanics and natural shoe angle; right-foot lead stays unresolved.
- **Run:** **Real alternating individual knees at waist height**, not merely different labels or the same raised knee in two shots. V1 ID34 fails. **V2 solves the still-image physical-leg ownership** with orange (near) and teal (far) ankle markers: upper left and lower left now belong to different knees. V3 clean edit was separately inspected and retains apparent geometry while deleting bands; keep tagged V2 as the lineage verification artifact.

# 5BX photographic pilot — round 2 mechanics and youth-identity audit

2026-11-26 UTC. Experimental stills only, **not a release or video-form approval**. The original 1959 RCAF 5BX instructions/film remain the exercise-form reference, and the approved middle-school photographic reference sheet remains the visual identity reference.

## Human safety correction, highest priority

- **29E Chart 3 V/forehead push-up: SAFETY HOLD.** A later human review identified unnaturally cocked, apparently hypermobile elbows in the deep forehead-to-hand phase. The old `STRONG CONDITIONAL` wording in older contact sheets is superseded. Do not feed 29E into Google Flow/Grok or use it for instruction.
- The previous `almost there` natural pike is preferred to a forced perfect forehead touch. A shorter, naturally achievable movement with normal shoulder→elbow→wrist geometry is a better demonstration than a deeper but implausible posture.
- A proposed 29F shorter-range edit is an **independent diagnostic test**, not automatically a replacement. Each individual phase must show plausible near/far joint alignment. A hidden elbow means `uncertain`, never automatic pass.
- **Accept/reject order:** joint integrity and contact/support correctness → ordinary 13–14-year-old anatomy → identifiable physical limb switching and exercise intent → cosmetic style and exact fingertip/forehead target. Never compensate for limited flexibility with extra height, elongated limbs, or hyperextending a joint.

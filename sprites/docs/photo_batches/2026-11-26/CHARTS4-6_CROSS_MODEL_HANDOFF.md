## Image prompting recipe to transfer

1. **One whole 2×2 or 3–4 phase card**, with the same person/camera/lighting/scale, usually succeeds better than four separately rendered children. On every phase identify exactly which limbs are planted, airborne, and where elbows/hands aim. For jumps explicitly say **natural athletic feet, no toe-pointing**.
2. **Physical limb ownership marker when needed:** do not equate screen-left with anatomical left. Use narrow **ORANGE band on physical near/right ankle (+ near/right elbow/wrist if upper body must cross)** and **TEAL on physical far/left ankle (+ far/left elbow/wrist)**. Specify each colored joint chain remains attached to the SAME hip and shoulder throughout. For 35R, B lifts teal left knee toward orange right elbow; C lifts orange right knee toward teal left elbow. For 34, A lifts orange near knee, C teal far knee. **Check a tagged render before removal**.
3. **One-variable clean-up**: reference the proven marker card; request restoration of dark socks and normal wrist only, no change of leg lineage, chest orientation, hand placement, camera or background. Inspect the *new pixels* independently; tags do not guarantee correction. Keep the tagged source in the audit trail.
4. **Authentic reference substitution for hard poses**: a fixed palm-line marker failed C3 forehead-pike ID29B. A real original RCAF pose photo plus a near-correct previous teen pose and separate identity master produced 29C/29D/29E. Prefer photo-based pose authority over repeated anatomy prose when shape remains wrong.
5. **Subtle anatomy pass after pose works**: ID32R softened upper arms; 37R and 41R reduced implausibly high airborne torsos **without removing visible two-hand clap or chest contact**. Use a single targeted edit; never sacrifice correct contact, age, height or body shape for prettier contours.

## Suggested one-anchor video tests — untested prompts

These are *proposed* Flow/Grok instructions, **not known successful video prompts**. Avoid feeding four panel images in arbitrary order when the video system may reverse phases. Begin with a single high-quality start anchor of the preferred full card's starting phase if the UI supports that, and inspect actual output at normal speed and frame samples.

**C5 37R clap push-up:** “Fixed side-oblique camera and same ordinary 13–14-year-old boy, face/blue tank/gray shorts identical. One controlled toe-supported push-up: lower close to track, push sharply for a split-second airborne hand release, bring *both palms together once* for a quick clap near chest, return hands immediately to the same planted points, and lower/recover. Clap is instantaneous, NEVER a held prayer position. Feet planted on toes, hips aligned, no extra arms, no hovering freeze, no camera movement or age change. Return to starting position for a natural loop.”

**C6 41R chest-slap push-up:** “Fixed side-oblique camera, same ordinary 13–14-year-old boy/wardrobe/track. After a controlled toe-supported push-up, release *both* hands very briefly off the track and tap each palm to its corresponding left/right side of the shirt/chest simultaneously, not hands together. Immediately replace both palms on track and recover. Do not suspend torso in air or pause at chest-touch; no third hand, no flip of hand identity, no camera cuts, no bodybuilder physique, no change of age or clothes. Natural shoe support and closed loop.”

**C5 35R twisting sit-up:** “Locked 30–35° camera. Begin lying on back with straight extended legs and hands gently supporting head, sit up as LEFT far knee bends toward RIGHT near elbow with chest rotated in that direction; fully lower and straighten; repeat with RIGHT near knee bent and LEFT far elbow reaching across, then full recovery. Do not switch screen labels only—actual two different hips/knees must move. Normal teen range: elbows aim toward but need not artificially reach the knee. Both feet and arms anatomically intact; static red track, same child and outfit, no reverse-order interpolation.”

**Acceptance gate for each rendered video:** Real near/far physical limb exchange, appropriate hand/toe contacts and realistic motion speeds, correct temporal phase order, no invented held unsupported pose, face/hair/clothing and body scale across frames, fixed camera and background, no frame reversals, recoverable loop seam. Keep separate **source-image**, **generation-prompt**, **video**, **motion QA**, **identity QA**, and **human-review** statuses. Until verified, mark `PROPOSED` or `CONDITIONAL`, never `APPROVED`.

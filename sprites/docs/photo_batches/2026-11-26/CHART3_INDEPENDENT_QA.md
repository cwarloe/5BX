## Cross-card lessons, independently checked

1. **Specific rare-pose authority beat one more marker edit.** Original 29 placed head wrongly; 29B correctly painted a fixed palm stripe but failed to shift its forehead. 29C independently recreated a correct head-*behind*-palm endpoint with actual 1959/teen pose references while preserving normal anatomy. **29D then preserved the correct B-versus-C palm-side topology in a jointly generated four-card**, a meaningful test progression. This is visual *still* evidence, not proof an AI can animate that movement.
2. **Knee angle uses an included angle.** For 28, 110° is not a 110° bend from straight (which would be too deep); assistant estimates from two projected shin/thigh lines should be reported as approximate, not measured. V1, 28B, 28C and preferred 28D are all valuable controlled lessons, but only 28D is currently best still.
3. **Source approval and source role are separate.** v2 is face/age/wardrobe authority; the original RCAF athlete is movement geometry authority only. No photo single endpoint or attractive four-pose sheet is owner-approved video form.
4. **Current identity:** All preferred tests depict a plausible fair 13–14-year-old with softly undeveloped arms/legs, messy dark hair, blue tank, charcoal shorts, dark socks, gray/yellow shoes and static field/track. Face/hair/perspective differ slightly across independent cards; the boy is **plausibly the same** rather than pixel-locked. Near pure profile is recurrent and hides far-arm/far-leg details.
5. **Ranked next gates:** (a) **29E now resolves the 29D margin failure** while preserving both crucial opposite-side head poses; obtain one native high-res *left-facing high-plank start* referenced to approved v2 before a motion test, rather than upscaling a 627px crop and claiming extra source detail; (b) for 26, recrop shoes and modestly reduce knee flexion only if anatomy remains realistic; (c) test 28D heel-only rise in actual motion and independently check knee range; (d) 27 needs far-leg/camera validation if used for instruction. No Chart 3 video has passed motion, loop, or identity QA.

**Overall:** 27, 28D, and now **29E** are **strong conditional still candidates**; 29D remains a breakthrough reference but loses preferred status to the wider framed 29E.

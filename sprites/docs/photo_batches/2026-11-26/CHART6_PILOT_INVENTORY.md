## Reproducibility and limits

Verbatim image prompts plus one-person QA reports are archived in `chart6_ex2/39_C6_EX2_ExactPrompt.md`, `chart6_ex3/40_C6_EX3_ExactPrompt.md`, `chart6_ex4/41_C6_ChestSlapPushup_ExactPrompt.md`, `chart6_ex1/42_C6_EX1_ReverseClaspToeCircle_ExactPrompt.md`, `chart6_ex1/42B_C6_EX1_ReverseClaspToeCircle_StandingRefinementPrompt.md`, and associated `*_QA_RESULT.md`. Original 5BX booklet/chart text remains exercise-form authority rather than visually plausible image-generation conventions.

Strong-looking intermediate photos do **not** establish the full motion, elbow/hand contact, clocking of toe-circle direction, actual video foot paths, identical camera, or the final video loop seam. These cards are candidates for deliberately short, separately audited motion experiments, not release assets.

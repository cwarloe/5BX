## QA gates before approval

1. **Identity:** same boy in first/mid/last frames, ordinary youth proportions, no drift of shirt or shoes.
2. **Leg topology:** B left bent/right straight for entire clip; C right bent/left straight. Hips and both heels anchored.
3. **Cross-body ownership:** B only right elbow advances toward left knee; C only left elbow toward right knee. Far hand may hide naturally, no forced exposure.
4. **Motion:** upper back lowers and rises, not a static freeze; no reverse-play artifacts or frame morphing.
5. **Loop:** final pose returns close to first without fake fade or hard pop.

Do not call it a 5BX instructional video until the mechanics are viewed frame by frame; a photo-accurate endpoint does not guarantee video-generated path correctness.

## Skeptical QA and remaining limits

- This is substantially more cross-body than 35R, especially B. C elbow is closer to the opposite knee, and C's upper chest rotates mildly; neither exact contact nor full 1950s form is proven.
- **Age/body:** Face/hair/ears appear consistent across same four-frame card. Exposed upper arms still somewhat defined, but no severe adult muscle growth or washboard abdomen. Watch for this when video-generating.
- **Geometry:** No obvious additional arm/leg, detached shoulder, double-jointed elbow, hyperextended spine, or foreground/background knee swap at visible size. Hands mostly at head, not countable; don't claim finger-level QA.
- **Pose order:** A supine → B right elbow to left knee → C left elbow to right knee → D supine. For actual animation, recommend two independent short cycles A→B→A and A→C→A; don't interpolate directly from B to C, because the planted and extended legs swap and the model may morph/reverse limbs.
- **Framing:** B is closer to side-on while C is more oblique, so **not phase-registered** for an unreviewed B↔C video. Existing 35R frame registration issue remains.
- **Comparisons:** `35R_vs_35T_CrossElbow_Zoom_Mobile.jpg` shows B/C anatomy close-up. The lower knee/foot location in C remains near-identical, with leg ownership auditable in 35S marker card.

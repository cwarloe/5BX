## Reusable prompt lessons

1. **Cross-frame limb ownership matters more than single-frame silhouette.** For any gait or high-knee sequence, label foreground/background arm and leg physically, then assert that the same limb changes positions over time. A beautiful isolated arm can be a failed animation keyframe.
2. When a previous card is already mostly good, **select only one panel, only one limb**, and explicitly name the protected oppositely moving limb. Check against the unedited trusted source, never an already rejected edit.
3. **Don't demand anatomically extreme positions from a young adolescent.** Permit a partial V/forehead-free endpoint as a success. A natural arm and neck angle is more important than forehead-to-floor precision.
4. Photograph fidelity is a rendering test, **not proof** of plausible or safe joint motion. Compare individual elbow hinge direction, forearm length, shoulder attachment, head/neck connection, and age consistency.
5. Keep all rejects as provenance with clear rejection status, not as default gallery/video inputs. Source `34A` bands are an analysis tool, not an output aesthetic.

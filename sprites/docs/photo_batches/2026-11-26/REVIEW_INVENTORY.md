## What is genuinely learned

1. Joint-generation avoids large face/age/color changes inside a successful four-phase card; that does **not** prove inter-card or video identity. The new v2 kneeling card is very similar to previous 09, with slightly darker blue, less fluffy hair, subtly softer face, but no major resolution improvement.
2. Chart 1's **partial** sit-up is a different exercise from the full sit-up previously animated for a later chart. Prompting 'sit-up' without the Chart 1 range would silently change the lesson.
3. Both the rejected scissor test **and** Chart1 alternate-leg raise repeat an established failure mode: a text description of inverse leg roles may still generate the same visible leg in each phase. Even switching the prone leg-raise view to 30° rear oblique (which truly reveals two feet) did not produce a verified leg swap. Creating independent poses does not solve frame registration or anatomical leg ownership.
4. Directly cropped contact-sheet tiles are only ~724×543 or ~883×438. Separately generated 1672×941 full-frame sources (Ex2 and Ex4) preserve substantially more visible fabric/face/shoe detail, at a cost of small face/age/hair drift in the higher-fidelity Ex4 photo. Do not claim post-crop interpolation increases source fidelity.
5. The camera remains **static**. Wide framing for the running/hopping sequence is intentional, not an image-quality defect.

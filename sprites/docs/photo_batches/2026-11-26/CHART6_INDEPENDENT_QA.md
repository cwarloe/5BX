## Original wording and avoidable pose misunderstandings

- **Pike:** “Sit up and at the same time lifting both legs to touch the toes in a pike (V) position.” Raising just one leg or leaving legs on ground would fail; ID39 does raise two, but toe contact itself remains missed. No anatomy stretching to force the literal touch.
- **Back extension with press:** “Raise arms, head, chest and both legs as high as possible then press back once.” This differs from Chart 5 because of **one extra controlled pulse**. ID40's B versus C higher arch suggests it, while still-frame interpolation cannot prove actual one-count pulse.
- **Chest-slap push-up:** “Push off floor and slap chest before returning.” Hands must **both leave the track** and touch chest, not each other; ID41 achieves the critical screenshot but doesn't demonstrate temporal ballistic physics.
- **Reverse-clasp circle:** “Feet astride, arms upward, hands reverse clasped, arms straight”; touch outside left, between feet with one press, outside right, then circle bend backward, reverse direction after half counts. Never replace clasp with two independent floor hands. Actual enlarged crop **confirms C outside-right reach**, but D's outward-facing interlocked hands/backward arch need better evidence; the final reverse-direction instruction cannot fit as a certified four-frame loop without additional phases/video.
- **Chart 6 jack jump is not among IDs 39–42** in this batch (ID42 is Exercise 1). Do not describe these tests as covering the last jump or claim its straight legs/airborne toe touch were validated.

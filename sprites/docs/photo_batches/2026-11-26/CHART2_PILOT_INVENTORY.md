**Cross-chart discipline:** A coherent four-panel photograph helps maintain age, clothes and background *within* the card; it does not verify video motion or exactly match face pixels *between* independently made cards. Keep the 35mm-style soft, normal 13–14-year-old anatomy, fixed camera and natural running shoes. Choose viewpoint per movement: slight front-oblique for astride, but adjust prone/supine off true 90° when far-side anatomy matters. All video tests should begin from one strong correctly framed anchor and be evaluated independently for motion, historical form and identity.

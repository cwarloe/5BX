## Known residual differences on this card

The owner approved the card as a working reference even with these leftover differences from the master. Do not copy them forward, and do not treat them as updates to the master.

1. Hair on several generated panels is a little fuller, curlier, and more uniformly dark than the master, with fewer warm highlights, especially in profile (panels 5 and 6, and some standing views).
2. Shoe yellow trim is slightly stronger than the master's muted detailing in some standing views (panels 2 and 4).
3. Loaded poses (panels 7 and 9) show slight upper-arm definition that the master does not have.
4. Independent face close-ups can look slightly broader or more symmetrical than the master close-up. The face-compare sheet is for spotting this drift, not for replacing the master crop.

## Known drift failures to reject

These failure modes are already recorded in the identity QA notes. Reject a new still or clip that shows them, even if the rest of the image looks close.

1. **Older or athletic teen:** a longer, more angular profile, a sharper chin or jaw, a prominent nose, or defined deltoids and triceps.
2. **Hair volume or curl:** a fluffy, curly crown mop, or kneeling hair that becomes fuller and more curled than the master.
3. **Hair too tidy, dark, or straight:** a styled or swept top, or darker, straighter, smoother hair than the master.
4. **Palette drift:** a paler tank, blue-gray shorts, or a warm, tanned skin cast.
5. **Shoes:** heavy or bright yellow stripes, or white shoes.
6. **Wristband:** missing, doubled, or on the left arm.
7. **Waist:** a narrowed, stretched, or broad-tapered torso.

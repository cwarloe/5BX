Fixing a pose by editing

The image tool can be told "change this, keep that". Untouched figures kept 96 to 99 percent of their shape, at about a quarter of the cost of regenerating.

Make the strips with any tool

Every prompt, the settings that worked, what went wrong and the fix are published, so the strips can be made with other image tools, possibly more cheaply. That includes prompts for matching background layers in six settings.

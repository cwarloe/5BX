TITLE
A Classic Fitness Plan in Your Pocket
The RCAF 5BX Plan as an installable, private, offline phone app.
ACT 1: SETUP
Setting Role
You want a short, structured daily workout that needs no gym or equipment. You're someone who wants to follow a proven plan and track progress.
Point A (Where am I?) Call to Action (How do I get from A to B?) Point B (Where do I want to be?)
Printed charts are easy to lose, and most fitness apps want accounts and connectivity. Install the app, follow today's workout, and log it. A fitness routine that sticks, on your own device.
Transition 15BX brings the 1961 plan to your phone. Three ideas explain it: the plan itself, the data behind it, and the simple build.

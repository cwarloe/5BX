TITLE
A Classic Fitness Plan in Your Pocket
The RCAF 5BX Plan as an installable, private, offline phone app.
ACT 1: SETUP
Setting Role Point A
You want a short, structured daily workout that needs no gym or equipment. You're someone who wants to follow a proven plan and track progress. Printed charts are easy to lose, and most fitness apps want accounts and connectivity.
TRANSITION 1
5BX brings the 1961 plan to your phone. Three ideas explain it: the plan itself, the data behind it, and the simple build.
